SwaraPaadam – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

SwaraPaadam ("the app") is a practice companion for Carnatic music: a shruti selector, a tambura, a talam (rhythm) counter, a live pitch display and a recorder. It is made by S. Dayalamurthy ("I", "me").

In short

The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. It does not send your voice, your recordings or any other personal data to me or to anyone else. Everything it does happens on your phone.

Microphone

The app asks for permission to use the microphone for two things:

  • Live pitch: it listens to the sound around you to show the frequency and the swara you are singing or playing. The sound is analysed on the phone in short pieces and then discarded. It is not saved and not sent anywhere.
  • Recording: when you press Record, the app records your microphone together with the tambura and talam into a video of the screen. This recording stays on your phone.

You can turn the live pitch off by tapping the pitch circle, and you can refuse or withdraw the microphone permission in your phone's settings. The tambura, talam and shruti still work without it.

Your recordings

A recording is kept inside the app until you record again (only the latest recording is kept) or close the app. If you choose Save, the file is written to your phone's Downloads/SwaraPaadam folder. If you choose Share, the file is handed to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp), and from then on that app's own privacy policy applies. I never receive the file.

Information stored on your phone

The app remembers your settings on the phone only: your chosen shruti, tambura and talam settings, and whether the live pitch is on. This is not sent anywhere. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes it.

Purchase

The shruti, tambura, talam and live pitch are free. Recording (and so sharing a recording) is unlocked by a one-time purchase made through Google Play. The payment, your payment details and your purchase record are handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account has bought the unlock, and remembers the answer on your phone. I do not receive your name, email address, payment details or any other personal data.

Internet

The app works offline and contains its own fonts. It has no server of its own and sends me nothing. It connects to the internet only to ask Google Play about your purchase, and when you choose to share a recording through another app such as WhatsApp.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date above.
